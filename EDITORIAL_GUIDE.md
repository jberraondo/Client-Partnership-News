# HSBC Partnership News: what we include

Claude reads this guide to decide which stories to **keep** or **drop** before
writing any bullets. It's based on past editions of the bulletin.

**To improve it:** paste more past bulletins into the "Past headlines" lists.
The more real examples there are, the better the keep/drop judgement gets.

The bulletin goes to a senior HSBC client audience. Every story should be one a
sports-business or sponsorship professional would want to know about today.

---

## Never included (any section)

- Local, grassroots or club-level news: a golf course getting a grant, a local
  club's fundraiser or new clubhouse, school or county results.
- Equipment, product or gear reviews, buying guides and "best of" lists.
- Betting tips, odds, predictions, fantasy picks, quizzes and puzzles.
- Opinion columns, rankings of players or "talking points" pieces with no new facts.
- Match previews or team-news pieces that only repeat known information.
- Live blogs, video or highlights pages, galleries and podcasts.
- Celebrity, lifestyle or gossip pieces.
- The same story already covered by another outlet. Keep the best version,
  preferring the section's preferred sources.

## Competitor News

**Keep:** one of the tracked banks (see SUMMARY_STYLE.md) doing a sponsorship,
partnership, naming-rights or activation deal in **sport or the arts**, anywhere
in the world. That includes new deals, renewals, extensions, exits, athlete
programmes, art collections, exhibitions, festivals and cultural partnerships.

**Drop:** earnings, share prices, takeovers, job cuts, regulation, general
banking products, and any company that isn't on the bank list.

## Wider Sports News

**Keep:** sports-industry and trade news, which doesn't need to involve HSBC:
rights deals, sponsorship and naming-rights announcements, media partnerships,
event hosting and bids, governing-body decisions, league and club commercial
moves, and agency or brand moves.

**Drop:** match reports, transfer gossip and player news (unless it's a major
commercial story), and general business or politics.

Past headlines:
- Sportcal: McDonald's in as inaugural naming partner for new Chicago Fire stadium

---

## Rugby

**Covers sevens and rugby union only**, men's and women's. Rugby league and
other codes are dropped.

**Keep:** HSBC SVNS Series events, results, standings and squads; international
rugby union (Six Nations, Rugby World Cup, autumn and summer tests, Red Roses);
top-flight club news (Premiership, URC, Top 14, Champions Cup) where it's
significant; major player news such as retirements, long injuries, captaincy and
selection; and World Rugby governance.

**Drop:** amateur, school or community rugby; routine minor-league results; rugby league.

Past headlines:
- BBC Sport: Wales and Scarlets lock Ball retires aged 34

---

## Racket (tennis and badminton)

**Keep (tennis):** Emma Raducanu (always relevant), Jack Draper and other leading
British players; HSBC-sponsored events such as Queen's Club; Grand Slam and
major ATP/WTA tournament news, results, draws and rankings; and significant
governing-body news.

**Keep (badminton):** HSBC BWF World Tour events, results, draws and rankings;
leading players at major tournaments; and BWF governance.

**Drop:** challenger or ITF-level results with no British or HSBC angle, club
tennis, padel or pickleball (unless it's a major commercial story), equipment and
coaching tips.

Past headlines:
- BBC Sport: Raducanu to make return with wildcard in Strasbourg
- ESPN: Sindhu, Lakshya, Sat-Chi reach Thailand Open quarters

---

## Golf

**Keep:** LIV Golf (events, players, the league's future), Bryson DeChambeau,
HSBC-sponsored events (e.g. Abu Dhabi HSBC Championship), majors, the PGA Tour,
DP World Tour, LPGA and Ryder Cup: results, leading-player news, fitness and
injuries, and tour governance and commercial news.

**Drop:** golf course grants, openings or renovations; club and amateur golf;
equipment reviews; course guides, travel and lifestyle pieces.

Past headlines:
- BBC Sport: McIlroy 'totally fine' for US PGA after toe issue
