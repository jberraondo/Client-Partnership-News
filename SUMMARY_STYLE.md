# HSBC Partnership News: summary bullet style

These are the rules Claude follows when it writes the two bullets for each story.
Edit this file to change the style. No code changes are needed.

## The two bullets

- Exactly **two bullets** per story, **100–150 words in total** across both.
- Each bullet is two to three sentences: fact-dense and closely paraphrased from the article.
- Keep the specific figures, names, dates, venues, competitions and deal lengths.
- **Bullet one** covers the core news: who, what, when, and the key numbers.
- **Bullet two** covers a different angle from the same story, such as background, context, what happens next, or a quote paraphrased into reported speech.
- British English spelling.
- No editorialising, hype or opinion ("huge", "stunning", "massive boost"). Report the facts.
- Don't start a bullet with the headline repeated back.
- Use only facts from the article itself. Never invent or guess details.

## Example

Headline: *Raducanu to make return with wildcard in Strasbourg* (BBC Sport)

1. Emma Raducanu will return to competitive action at the Strasbourg clay-court event after being granted a wildcard, having been absent since a third-round defeat at Indian Wells on 8 March following a post-viral infection.
2. The Strasbourg event begins on Sunday, one week before the French Open on 24 May. Raducanu is set to enter Roland Garros unseeded as a result of her absence from the tour.

## Competitor News: what counts

Competitor News tracks **these banks only** doing sponsorship or partnership
deals in sport or the arts, anywhere in the world:

Barclays, Lloyds, Santander, NatWest, Metro Bank, Citi (Citibank), Revolut,
Monzo, Chase / JPMorganChase / J.P. Morgan, Standard Chartered, BNP Paribas,
Deutsche Bank.

Examples of stories that belong:

- Monzo becomes official banking partner of The Hundred (cricket)
- JPMorganChase becomes the Dallas Mavericks' jersey patch and banking partner
- Lloyds and British Cycling launch the Sofa to Saddle app
- JPMorgan agrees a $15m-a-year jersey patch deal with Ohio State
- Miami HEAT and JPMorganChase enter a multi-year partnership
- Revolut and MOJOE launch an athlete career-transition programme
- Lloyds becomes England Rugby's first back-of-shirt partner

New deals, renewals, extensions, exits and activations all count. Other banks,
payment companies (Visa, Mastercard, Adyen, PayPal, Amex), takeovers, earnings,
general banking news and HSBC's own deals don't count.

**For every story in every section**, set `"competitor": true` if the story is
mainly about a deal like this. It will then be moved into Competitor News. For
example, a Sportcal story in Wider Sports News about a bank renewing a club
sponsorship should be flagged.

## When to skip a story

Mark a story as skipped (no bullets) if any of these apply:
- It isn't a real news article, for example a video page, gallery, live blog, podcast, product review, betting tips or a homepage.
- **Competitor News** only: the story doesn't meet the definition above.
- **Wider Sports News** only: the story isn't about sport or the business of sport (rights, sponsorship, media, events, governing bodies). General business, politics or lifestyle pieces are skipped.
